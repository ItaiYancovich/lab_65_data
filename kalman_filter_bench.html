Every prediction widens the belief; every measurement narrows it again. The two lines close on each other as the filter reaches steady state — the point where one reading's worth of information exactly offsets one step of drift. Switch on sensor dropout to tear the gap open.

Kalman gain Kp

0 — ignore the sensor, trust the model. 1 — jump to the reading, discard the model. The filter settles wherever those two errors balance.

Constant-velocity model, state x = [position, velocity], scalar position measurement. Process noise uses the discrete white-noise-acceleration form Q = σa² · [[dt⁴/4, dt³/2], [dt³/2, dt²]]. The target's true motion is a sinusoidal acceleration plus a random walk and a little drag, so the model is deliberately imperfect — as it always is in a real lab. At the default tuning the estimate stays inside its own ±2σ band about 91% of the time, a shade under the nominal 95%: the price of that mismatch.
